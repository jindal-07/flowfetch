        How FlowFetch works

        Pick a source at the top (Facebook, SharePoint, or Google Drive), then choose how you want to fetch. Everything streams straight from the source through this server to your browser — nothing is stored on the server.

        Facebook
        • Single link — paste a video or Reel URL and get the MP4. Short links (fb.watch, /share/) are resolved automatically.
        • CSV upload — upload a CSV with a Links column (or URLs in the first column) to download in bulk.
        SharePoint
        • Sign in first — use Sign in with Microsoft. Running locally, you can also do a browser login or upload a state.json. The auth status shows at the top of the tab.
        • Single link / CSV upload — download one share link or a whole CSV of them.
        • Folder → CSV — paste a folder share link (the :f: kind). FlowFetch crawls it recursively and hands you a manifest CSV of every file. Re-upload that CSV under “CSV upload” to download the files.
        Google Drive
        • No sign-in — works with files and folders shared as “Anyone with the link.”
        • Single link / CSV upload — grab one file or a CSV of Drive links.
        • Folder → CSV — paste a folder link to build a manifest CSV, then re-upload it to download everything inside.
        Good to know
        • Live progress — a progress bar and log stream as work happens; a summary of success / failed / skipped appears at the end.
        • Batches — bulk downloads over 100 files split into equal ZIP batches (e.g. 250 → 84 + 83 + 83) that save one after another.
        • Stop anytime — the Stop button cancels after the current file finishes.
        • Retry failures — download a CSV of just the failed links to run again.
